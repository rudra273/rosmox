## Storely prompt

Use case: compositing / ads-marketing.
Asset type: a finished 16:9 landscape product banner for the ROSMOX website, ideally 2048x1152.
Primary request: create a sophisticated Storely mobile-app banner using the three supplied real screenshots as the actual screen contents.
Input images: 1 Storely home dashboard; 2 product inventory; 3 billing. These are supporting compositing inputs, not inspiration for invented interfaces. Preserve the actual interface, typography, screen labels, amounts and proportions. Remove only the Android status and system navigation bars from the phone presentation.
Composition: generous editorial type area occupying left third; on the right three elegant front-facing slim dark phone mockups in a restrained stepped arrangement, with home foremost and products/bills on either side. Show all phones completely inside the frame with generous margins, no cropping. Phones should occupy most of frame height so screens remain recognizable. Subtle studio shadows, no perspective distortion of UI.
Backdrop: deep near-black graphite with a restrained soft warm amber glow behind phones. Colors mirror the screenshots.
Text (verbatim) in left area: "Storely" as main large title, then "Billing. Inventory. Your store." in a readable smaller line or two. Small category "RETAIL MANAGEMENT". Use simple modern geometric sans typography, white and warm amber. No other marketing text, no badges.
Constraints: accurate supplied screenshot UI; no fictional features, customer claims, statistics, testimonials, logos, watermarks, extra decorative objects. Match a polished minimal technology portfolio. One finished single banner only.
